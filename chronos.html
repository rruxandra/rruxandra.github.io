Complete Lifespan Synthesis (Age 15 to 85)

GH Recovery, Dopamine Novelty, Plasticity & Conscientiousness

1. High Plasticity & Novelty (15–28)

Growth hormone, striatal dopamine receptor sensitivity (novelty-seeking), and raw fluid neuroplasticity peak. However, prefrontal myelination and Big-5 Conscientiousness are at their lowest—meaning high exploration drive, but lower natural self-regulation.

2. The Great Crossover (30–48)

Around age 32–36, the Conscientiousness curve intersects and surpasses tapering raw novelty sensitivity (~5–8% decline per decade in striatal D2/D3 receptors). Forming long-term habits and routines becomes neurologically easier, though individual variation and lifestyle strongly modulate tonic dopamine.

3. Crystallized Mastery (50–85)

Crystallized intelligence (mental models, vocabulary, strategic synthesis) and emotional equanimity (lower amygdala reactivity to stress) reach their zenith. Optimization shifts to preserving cellular recovery/sleep while leveraging high wisdom and established habits.
