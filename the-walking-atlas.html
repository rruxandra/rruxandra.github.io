a closing thought

Not search. Cartography.

Search is a single question, asked once, answered narrowly, forgotten. A map is a thousand questions, asked ambiently, answerable at a glance, present even when you are not looking.

The library never needed a catalog. It needed a cartographer — quiet enough to walk it without disturbing it, and patient enough to never stop.

The thing about a map
is that it knows where you are.
The map of your life ought to know too.
